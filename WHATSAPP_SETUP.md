# WhatsApp Notification Setup Guide

Follow these steps to get your Meta Cloud API keys:

1.  **Login**: Click **Login** in the top right of [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com) using your Facebook account.
2.  **Create App**: If you don't have an app, click the **"My Apps"** link (usually appears after login) and then **"Create App"**.
    - **App Type**: Choose **"Other"**.
    - **Experience**: Choose **"Business"**.
3.  **Add WhatsApp**: On the Add Product screen, find **WhatsApp** and click **Set Up**.
4.  **Quickstart**: You will see a "Getting Started" page. Look for:
    - **Temporary Access Token** (for testing right now)
    - **Phone Number ID** (a long number)
    - **WhatsApp Business Account ID**

Paste those 3 items here when you find them! I'll handle the coding part in the meantime.
